Stepping into a modern specialty coffee shop often means facing a menu full of unfamiliar names. For anyone used to traditional drip coffee or instant brews, choosing the right drink can feel intimidating.

Let's break down the most popular menu items so you can order exactly what you are craving during your next visit in Torrevieja or Alicante.

---

### 1. The Foundation of Everything: Espresso
The entire universe of craft coffee revolves around espresso. It is a small serving (approx. 25-30 ml) of concentrated coffee extracted under about 9 bars of pressure in a short time (20-30 seconds).

* **Taste Profile:** Intense, balanced, combining sweetness, acidity, and a delicate bitterness, topped with a dense layer of crema.
* **Who it's for:** Lovers of pure, bold coffee and a quick energy boost.

### 2. Milk Classics: Flat White, Cappuccino, and Caffè Latte
All of these drinks consist of espresso and textured milk, but they differ in proportions and structure:

* **Cappuccino:** An Italian classic (usually 150-180 ml). It consists of a single shot of espresso paired with velvety textured milk and a characteristic fluffy foam on top. The ratio of coffee, milk, and foam is well-balanced.
* **Flat White:** Originating from Australia and New Zealand, this drink is designed for fans of a stronger coffee kick. It is usually prepared with a **double espresso (doppio)** and a smaller amount of microfoam milk. The coffee flavor stands out distinctively through the milk layer.
* **Caffè Latte:** The largest of the milk classics (often over 250 ml). It consists of a single espresso and a generous amount of hot milk with a thin layer of foam. An ideal choice for a slow, relaxed sip.

---

### 3. Alternative Brewing Methods (Filter Coffee)
If you prefer a larger, black coffee with a rich body and clean flavor profile, look for menu items like **Drip V60, Chemex**, or **Aeropress**.

* **How they are made:** Instead of pressure, gravity does the work. Hot water slowly filters through a paper filter containing medium-coarsely ground coffee beans.
* **Taste Profile:** Exceptionally rich – depending on the beans (e.g., from Ethiopia or Kenya), you can detect notes of citrus, bergamot, jasmine, or red berries, completely free of harsh bitterness.
* **Who it's for:** Fans of discovering subtle, fruity nuances in coffee.

---

### 4. Refreshment for Warm Days: Cold Brew and Espresso Tonic
The Costa Blanca climate calls for refreshing iced coffee drinks.

* **Cold Brew:** Coarsely ground coffee steeped in cold water and refrigerated for 12 to 24 hours. It yields a smooth, naturally sweet brew with low acidity and a high caffeine kick.
* **Espresso Tonic:** A summer hit – a double espresso poured over ice cubes and tonic water, often garnished with a slice of lemon or lime. A refreshing, tart-sweet combination.

---

### Summary
Choosing the right drink depends on what you need right now: go for an **espresso** for a quick energy boost, a **flat white** or **cappuccino** to accompany your breakfast or work session, and a **pour-over** to leisurely savor delicate fruity notes!
