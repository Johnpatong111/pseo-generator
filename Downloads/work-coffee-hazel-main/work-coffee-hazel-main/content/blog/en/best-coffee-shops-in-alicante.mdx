Alicante is famous for its Mediterranean beaches, palm-lined esplanades, and over 300 days of sunshine a year. But for digital nomads and coffee lovers, this vibrant coastal city has built a thriving **specialty coffee scene**.

Whether you need an early espresso boost or a laptop-friendly corner with reliable Wi-Fi, here are the top coffee spots in Alicante:

---

### 1. Sip & Wonder Coffee House – Specialty Coffee & Fresh Brunch
Located near the *Mercado Central*, **Sip & Wonder** is a staple for locals and expats. They offer freshly baked sourdough bread, artisanal breakfasts, and carefully sourced coffee beans.

- **Why visit:** Outstanding pour-over methods (V60, AeroPress) and a bright, airy space.
- **Best for:** A slow weekend brunch or a productive morning work session.

### 2. Madness Specialty Coffee – Industrial Vibe in Old Town
If you appreciate industrial aesthetics and in-house roasting, **Madness Coffee** in the *Casco Antiguo* district will captivate you. Its steampunk-inspired interior and dedicated roasting setup create a unique experience.

- **Why visit:** Perfect Flat Whites, single-origin beans, and solid Wi-Fi upstairs.
- **Best for:** Coffee purists and creatives seeking an inspiring atmosphere.

### 3. Swallow Specialty Coffee – Cozy Gem in the City Center
Situated at the picturesque *Plaza del Carmen*, **Swallow** is a charming, intimate cafe focused on quality extraction and healthy homemade treats.

- **Why visit:** Peaceful atmosphere, friendly baristas, and great attention to detail.
- **Best for:** A quick afternoon coffee break or quiet reading.

---

### Pro Tip for Digital Nomads in Alicante:
Breakfast rush hour in Spain (*desayuno*) runs between 9:30 AM and 11:00 AM. If you want a quiet table with a power outlet, try arriving right at opening time or in the early afternoon!
